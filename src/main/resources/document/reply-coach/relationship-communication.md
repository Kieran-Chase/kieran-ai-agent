# Reply Coach - Relationship Communication

## Core principle

Relationship communication is not about winning an argument. It is about expressing feelings, needs, and boundaries clearly.

## Recommended style

Use "I feel...", "I hope...", "I cannot accept..." instead of "You always..." or "You never...".

## Apology structure

1. Admit the concrete behavior.
2. Acknowledge the impact on the other person.
3. Do not rush to defend yourself.
4. Say what you will change.

## Conflict pause template

"We are both emotional now. If we continue, we may hurt each other more. Let's pause first and discuss the specific issue later."

## Chinese response guidance

For Chinese users, give replies that sound like real chat messages, not formal essays. Provide versions for partner, friend, family, and roommate when needed.

## Common situations

- Apologizing
- Explaining a misunderstanding
- Expressing discomfort
- Asking for emotional support
- Ending an argument
- Rebuilding trust after conflict
